Dhruva was the son of King Uttanapada. Uttanapada had two wives, named Suruchi and Suneethi. Suruchi was a very pompous and cruel person. She had a son named Uttama. Suruchi was hell-bent on outshining Suneethi in anything she did. On one occasion, when Uttanapada was sitting on the throne in the throne room, Dhruva approached Uttanapada and asked him if he could sit on his lap.
