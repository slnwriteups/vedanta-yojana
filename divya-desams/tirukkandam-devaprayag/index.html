Tirukkandam (Devaprayag)

Temple Information

Moolavar
Neelamegha Perumāl
Thayaar
Pundareegavalli Nāchiyār
Vimanam
Mangala Vimānam
Theertham
Mangala Tīrtham
How to reach
This kshethram is located in Devaprayag en route to Badrināth.

Sthala Puranam

According to legend, Lord Rāma performed tapas here at this kshethram to nullify the brahmahatyadosham for killing Rāvaṇa. Lord Rāma is also said to have bathed in the Gaṅgā River and performed tarpanam for His forefathers here at this kshethram.

Azhwar Pasuram
