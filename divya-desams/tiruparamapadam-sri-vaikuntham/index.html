Tiruparamapadam (Shri Vaikuṇṭham)

Temple Information

Moolavar
Shri Paramapadanathan
Thayaar
Shrīdevī, Bhūdevi, Neeladevi
Vimanam
Ananthanga Vimānam
Theertham
Viraja Nadi Tīrtham

Sthala Puranam

This kshethram is the ultimate destination for any jeevaatma. This kshethram lies beyond Tiruparkadal. Shri Vaikuṇṭham is described as a mesmerizing place from which there is no return, as specified in the Vedas and Upanishads as "na cha punara avarthathe na cha punara avarthathe na cha punara avarthathe." The Lord is seen as Shri Paramapadanathan, sitting alongside Him atop Ādisheshan are Shrīdevī, Bhūdevi, and Neeladevi. After the jeevaathma crosses Ksheerabdi, it will be guided to the borders of Shri Vaikuṇṭham. Here flows the Viraja River.

The jeevaathma is allowed to bathe in the Viraja River, where the divine river washes away any residual sins. Upon entering Shri Vaikuṇṭham, the jeevaathma will see all the gods singing the praise of the Lord, which is described as "tad viṣṇoḥ paramaṁ padaṁ sadā paśyanti sūrayaḥ." After being mesmerized by all of this, the jeevaathma will go and finally see the Lord Himself. The Lord gives space on his lap and asks the jeevaathma what took it so long to reach Him. The jeevaathma has no response but to say it has finally attained Him.

The jeevaathma then offers itself to become one with the Lord. It asks the Lord to give it space inside His Heart to attain moksham. The jeevaathma asks the Lord to imagine it to be a single grain of rice, and that grain is being offered solely and wholeheartedly to Him. The Lord then places the jeevaathma near His heart, merging the jeevaathma with Himself, the Paramātma, granting moksham.
