On one side, Bhoomadevi approaches the Lord seeking refuge at the Lord's tiruvadi. Shri Devī is seen near the head of Ādishesha, also seeking refuge. The Lord raises His hand to offer assurance to Shri Devī and lifts His gaze toward Bhoomadevi to reassure her.

The entire episode is captured within a single panel, carved in stone.
