Tiruvaali Tirunagari

Temple Information

How to reach
Tiruvaali is located 10 kilometers from Sīrkāzhi, and Tirunagari is located 5 kilometers from Tiruvaali.

Sthala Puranam

At Tiruvaali, we can see Lakshmī Narasimhar. It is said that after killing Hiraṇyakashipu, the Lord was still furious. To get the Lord to calm down all the devas, Lord Brahmā and Lord Shiva asked Mahālakshmī to isolate the Lord to a different location. Thus, the Lord was taken to Tiruvaali. Usually, we see that Lakshmī is seated on the Lord's left lap, but here, we can see Lakshmī seated on the Lord's right, allowing her to hug and embrace the Lord.

Thus, the place got its name Tiruvaali (Tiru meaning Lakshmī and Aali meaning Aalinganam or hug in Sanskrit).

*** Vedupari Utsavam

Tiruvaali is said to be the birthplace of Amritavalli Thāyār, and Tirunagari is said to be the birthplace of Lord Vayalali Māṇavāḷan. It is said that on the night that the Lord got married to Thāyār, they were targeted and looted by Tirumangai Āzhwār. Before becoming a saint, Tirumangai Āzhwār was a dacoit named Kaliyan.

Tirumangai Āzhwār wanted to marry a woman named Kumudhavalli. She placed two conditions, namely, 1) To get Pancha-Samskāram done, and 2) to perform tadhiyararadhanam (annadhanam) for a thousand people for one year. To satisfy the second condition, Tirumangai Āzhwār used to steal gold from anyone he could find, sell the gold to get money to serve people. As fate would have it, Tirumangai Āzhwār ran into the Lord and Thāyār. He pulled out a knife and told them to remove all the jewelry that they were wearing.

The Lord and Thāyār gave up all their jewelry. Tirumangai Āzhwār tied up all of the jewelry in a cloth, and as he was about to lift it, the Lord performed an enchantment in such a way that he could no longer lift it. In anger, Tirumangai Āzhwār threatened the Lord to give him the mantra that would allow him to lift the bag. The Lord smiled and told him that he was performing all of this to make sure that Tirumangai Āzhwār would obtain this mantra and propagate it to the world. Thus the Lord gave Tirumangai Āzhwār the ashtakshara mantram.

All of this is performed as a drama to date on Pankuni Uttaram day. Find the pictures of the utsavam attached below.
