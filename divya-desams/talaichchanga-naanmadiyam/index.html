How to reach
This kshethram is located in Talaichchangaadu village, near Aakkur, close to Sīrkāzhi, between Chidambaram and Māyilāduthurai.

Sthala Puranam

It is said that Chandran was the husband of the twenty-seven nakshatras, who were the daughters of Daksha Prajapati. It is noted that Chandran showed more affection towards Rohini and was ignoring his other twenty-six wives. Thus, Daksha Prajapati was angered by Chandran's behavior and cursed him, saying he would suffer from Kshaya-Rogam(tuberculosis). It is said that to relieve himself of the curse, Chandran came here to this kshethram and prayed to the Lord after taking a dip in the temple's pushkariṇī.

The Lord is then said to have appeared here and relieved Chandran of his curse.
