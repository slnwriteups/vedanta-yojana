Tirunindravur

Temple Information

Moolavar
Bhakthavatsala Perumāl
Thayaar
Sudhavalli / Ennaipetra Thāyār
Vimanam
Utpala Vimānam
Theertham
Varuṇa Pushkariṇī
How to reach
This kshethram is situated 25 km from Chennai, on the Chennai-Tiruvaloor Road.

Sthala Puranam

The legend has it that Varuṇa and Samudraraja worshipped Lord Vishṇu at this kshethram. It is also believed that the Lord visiting this kshethram, followed by Lakshmī, made it His home, and hence the name Triu Ninravoor. It is said that Tirumangai Āzhwār, after worshipping at this shrine, left without singing a pāsuram. The Lord followed him to Māmallapuram, where he sang the pāsuram, referring to this kshethram.

Azhwar Pasuram

Tirumangai Āzhwār: 2 Pāsurams
