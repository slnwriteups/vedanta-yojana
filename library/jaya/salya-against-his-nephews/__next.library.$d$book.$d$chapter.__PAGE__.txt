5:T763,King Shalya of Madra, the maternal uncle of Nakula and Sahadeva, heard that his nephews the Pāṇḍavas were preparing for war, and he mustered a great army and marched to join them. But Duryodhana learned of his coming, and devised a cunning trick to steal him away. Along the whole route of Shalya's march, Duryodhana had beautiful rest-houses built, and lavished upon Shalya and his soldiers such splendid feasts and hospitality that Shalya, assuming all this bounty came from his nephew Yudhishthira, was deeply moved, and called for the host to be brought so that he might reward him. And at that moment Duryodhana stepped forward and revealed that he himself was the host.
And Shalya, a man of honour, was stunned, but bound by his own gratitude, and asked how he might repay such hospitality. And Duryodhana at once demanded that Shalya fight on the Kaurava side. And Shalya, held fast by the ancient code that one who has eaten a host's food is indebted to grant his request, agreed, though he bitterly regretted that he had been tricked into betraying his own nephews.
Then Shalya went on to Upaplavya to confess his mistake to Yudhishthira. And Yudhishthira, hiding his disappointment, acknowledged that the promise was binding and must be kept. But he made a request, for he knew that Shalya was the equal of Krishna in the driving of a chariot, and that Duryodhana would surely make Shalya the charioteer of Karṇa in his final duel with Arjuna. When that hour comes, Yudhishthira asked, use your place to dishearten Karṇa, to deflate his pride, to weaken his spirit, and so protect Arjuna's life. And Shalya agreed: he would fight honourably on the Kaurava side, as his vow required, but he would use his tongue, as Karṇa's charioteer, to undermine Karṇa's confidence and so shield Arjuna. और शल्य, एक सम्मान के पुरुष, चकित रह गए, परंतु अपनी ही कृतज्ञता से बंधे हुए, पूछा कि वे ऐसे आतिथ्य का बदला कैसे चुका सकते हैं। और दुर्योधन ने तुरंत माँग की कि शल्य कौरव पक्ष से युद्ध करें। और शल्य, इस प्राचीन संहिता से जकड़े हुए कि जिसने आतिथेय का भोजन खाया है वह उसकी माँग पूरी करने का ऋणी है, सहमत हो गए, यद्यपि उन्हें कड़वाहट से पछतावा हुआ कि उन्हें अपने ही भांजों के साथ विश्वासघात में छला गया।

Duryodhana came first, and finding Krishna asleep, seated himself proudly at the head of the bed; Arjuna came shortly after, and stood humbly at the foot, his hands folded in reverence.
When Krishna woke, his eyes fell first upon Arjuna standing at his feet; then, turning, he saw Duryodhana at his head. Duryodhana at once claimed precedence, arguing that he had arrived first. But Krishna answered that though Duryodhana had come first, he himself had seen Arjuna first on waking, and so his help was owed equally to both. And he offered them a choice between two forms of aid: on one side his vast and undefeated army, the Nārāyaṇī Sena, thousands of veteran warriors; and on the other side Krishna himself, alone, unarmed, pledged never to lift a weapon or take part in the fighting. And because custom gave the younger man the first choice, he offered it to Arjuna.
And Arjuna, without a moment's hesitation, bowed and chose Krishna, unarmed though he would be. And Duryodhana could scarcely hide his delight at what he took for Arjuna's folly, and gladly claimed the mighty army for himself. And when Duryodhana had gone, Krishna asked Arjuna why he had chosen a weaponless non-combatant over an invincible host. And Arjuna answered: I do not doubt my own power to defeat the enemy; but I long for your presence, to guide my chariot and to light my path. The citizens thronged the streets to see the Lord; and Duryodhana, hoping to buy his favour, prepared lavish gifts and a grand feast. But Krishna passed by Duryodhana's palace and chose instead to lodge in the humble house of Vidura, and spent the night in quiet talk and in comforting Kuntī, who wept over the coming tragedy.
The next day Krishna entered the royal court, and declined Duryodhana's feast, saying famously that an envoy eats only after his mission is accomplished. And addressing the blind king Dhritarāshtra, he made a moving plea for peace, asking now not for half the kingdom, but for only five villages, one for each of the five brothers, that the whole quarrel might be settled honourably and the war averted. And Dhritarāshtra agreed with the plea, but confessed his utter helplessness over his own sons. Then Krishna turned to Duryodhana and urged him to cast off his envy and save his race from destruction; and Bhīshma and Droṇa and Vidura all joined in pressing him to yield. But Duryodhana, furious at being rebuked before the court, declared that the Pāṇḍavas had lost their kingdom fairly at play and that he would never yield to threats, that he would not give them even so much land as could be balanced on the point of a needle.
And more than refuse, Duryodhana and his associates plotted to seize Krishna himself and bind him prisoner. And Krishna, knowing their plan, laughed, and revealed in that hall his cosmic form, the Vishvarūpa, his single body containing the whole universe, all the worlds and all beings within him, a vision of overwhelming divinity. And the blind Dhritarāshtra, by the Lord's grace, was given sight for a moment to behold it; and having seen the Supreme thus, he begged to be made blind again, for he wished never after to look upon any lesser, worldly thing. ಮತ್ತು ನಿರಾಕರಿಸುವುದಕ್ಕಿಂತ ಹೆಚ್ಚಾಗಿ, ದುರ್ಯೋಧನ ಮತ್ತು ಅವನ ಸಹಚರರು ಸ್ವತಃ ಕೃಷ್ಣನನ್ನೇ ಹಿಡಿದು ಬಂಧಿಸಲು ಸಂಚು ಹೂಡಿದರು. ಮತ್ತು ಕೃಷ್ಣ, ಅವರ ಯೋಜನೆಯನ್ನು ತಿಳಿದು, ನಕ್ಕನು, ಮತ್ತು ಆ ಸಭೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ತನ್ನ ವಿಶ್ವರೂಪವನ್ನು, ತನ್ನ ಏಕ ಶರೀರದೊಳಗೆ ಇಡೀ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಾಂಡವನ್ನೂ, ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಲೋಕಗಳನ್ನೂ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಜೀವಿಗಳನ್ನೂ ಒಳಗೊಂಡ ರೂಪವನ್ನು, ಅಗಾಧವಾದ ದೈವತ್ವದ ಒಂದು ದರ್ಶನವನ್ನು ಬಹಿರಂಗಪಡಿಸಿದನು. ಮತ್ತು ಕುರುಡ ಧೃತರಾಷ್ಟ್ರನಿಗೆ, ಭಗವಂತನ ಅನುಗ್ರಹದಿಂದ, ಅದನ್ನು ಕಾಣಲು ಒಂದು ಕ್ಷಣ ದೃಷ್ಟಿ ನೀಡಲಾಯಿತು; ಮತ್ತು ಪರಮಾತ್ಮನನ್ನು ಹೀಗೆ ಕಂಡ ನಂತರ, ಅವನು ಮತ್ತೆ ಕುರುಡನನ್ನಾಗಿ ಮಾಡಿಕೊಳ್ಳಲು ಬೇಡಿಕೊಂಡನು, ಏಕೆಂದರೆ ಅದರ ನಂತರ ಯಾವುದೇ ಕೀಳಾದ, ಲೌಕಿಕ ವಸ್ತುವನ್ನೂ ತಾನು ಎಂದೂ ನೋಡಲು ಬಯಸಲಿಲ್ಲ. ಮತ್ತು ಕೃಷ್ಣ ಆ ದರ್ಶನವನ್ನು ಹಿಂತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡು ಸಭೆಯಿಂದ ಹೊರಟುಹೋದನು, ಅವನ ಶಾಂತಿಯ ಕಾರ್ಯಾಚರಣೆ ನಿರಾಕರಿಸಲ್ಪಟ್ಟಿತ್ತು; ಮತ್ತು ಅದರ ವೈಫಲ್ಯದೊಂದಿಗೆ, ಮಹಾ ಯುದ್ಧ ಈಗ ಯಾವುದೂ ತಡೆಯಲಾಗದ ಒಂದು ಖಚಿತತೆಯಾಯಿತು.10:T160b,युद्ध की सभी विशाल तैयारियों के बावजूद, कृष्ण ने संपूर्ण योद्धा वंश को निगल लेने वाली विपत्ति को रोकने का एक अंतिम गंभीर प्रयास करने का निश्चय किया, और वे स्वयं शांति के दूत के रूप में हस्तिनापुर गए। भगवान को देखने के लिए नागरिक गलियों में उमड़ पड़े; और दुर्योधन, उनकी कृपा पाने की आशा में, भव्य उपहार और एक शानदार भोज तैयार किया। परंतु कृष्ण दुर्योधन के महल से आगे निकल गए और इसके बजाय विदुर के विनम्र घर में ठहरना चुना, और शांत बातचीत में तथा आने वाली त्रासदी पर रोती हुई कुंती को सांत्वना देने में रात बिताई।
