There they lodged in a humble potter's cottage and went to the royal assembly disguised as young brāhmaṇas. Drupada had set an extraordinary test: a great stiff bow, and high above a rotating fish target behind a spinning wheel, and the archer must string the bow and send five arrows through the wheel into the fish's eye, aiming only by its reflection in a vessel of oil below.
Mighty kings and warriors tried and failed and were humbled, the stiff bow flinging them off; even Karṇa tried and failed by a hair. Then Arjuna, in his plain brāhmaṇa's deerskin, stepped out from among the priests; and meditating on the Lord, he lifted the great bow with ease, strung it without a tremor, and looking down at the reflection in the oil, sent five arrows through the spinning wheel into the target's eye, and it fell amid a rain of flowers. Draupadī set the garland of victory on Arjuna's neck.

For the town was terrorized by a cannibal demon, Bakāsura, and by an old agreement each household in turn must send him a cartload of food, two buffaloes, and a human being, whom he ate along with the rest; and now it was this family's turn. When Yudhishthira heard, he was alarmed, for it was on Bhīma's strength that all their hopes rested; why risk him? But Kuntī's answer stood on the highest ground. We have lived in this good man's house and eaten his bread, she said; gratitude is the highest of virtues, and to repay kindness with kindness is a sacred duty; and I know Bhīma's strength; he who carried us out of the flames will crush this demon. Mark Kuntī's reasoning, for it is the heart of the chapter. And the division was itself an insult, for to the Pāṇḍavas he gave Khāṇḍavaprastha, a barren stretch of desert and ancient wasteland, desolate and unwanted, while Duryodhana kept the rich and settled heart of the realm.
To the eye of the world it was a cruel bargain, the best of the princes fobbed off with a wilderness. But the Pāṇḍavas did not complain, and here the story shows its first great turn from misfortune to triumph, for with the guidance of Lord Krishna and the labour of celestial architects they took that wasteland and transformed it, clearing and building and raising, until where there had been only desert there stood a magnificent city, Indraprastha, one of the wonders of the age.
There is a quiet teaching in this reclaiming, and it is worth pausing on before the splendours overtake it. The Pāṇḍavas were given a desert and made of it a paradise; they did not curse the poverty of their portion but poured their strength into it and made it flower. And is this not the very task of a life? Each of us is given some barren ground, some portion we did not choose and would not have chosen, some wilderness of circumstance; and the measure of a soul is not the richness of the ground it is given but what it makes of the ground it has. The Pāṇḍavas took the worst land in the kingdom and made it the most beautiful, and in doing so they showed that the desolate places, met with the right spirit and the right help, can be made to bloom.
And the city held one marvel above the rest. Mayāsura, the great architect of the demons, whose life the Pāṇḍavas had spared, built for them a hall of assembly of such refined art that it confounded the senses: floors of polished crystal that looked like water, and pools of clear water that looked like solid floor, so that the eye could not tell the solid from the seeming. It was a wonder; and it was also, though none yet knew it, a snare, for the confusion it worked upon the senses would one day work upon a proud guest and kindle the quarrel that would burn down the world. ಮತ್ತು ಆ ನಗರವು ಎಲ್ಲಕ್ಕಿಂತ ಮಿಗಿಲಾದ ಒಂದು ಅದ್ಭುತವನ್ನು ಹೊಂದಿತ್ತು. ರಾಕ್ಷಸರ ಮಹಾ ವಾಸ್ತುಶಿಲ್ಪಿಯಾದ ಮಯಾಸುರ, ಪಾಂಡವರು ಯಾರ ಜೀವವನ್ನು ಉಳಿಸಿದ್ದರೋ, ಅವರಿಗಾಗಿ ಇಂದ್ರಿಯಗಳನ್ನು ಗೊಂದಲಗೊಳಿಸುವಷ್ಟು ಪರಿಷ್ಕೃತ ಕಲೆಯ ಒಂದು ಸಭಾ ಮಂಟಪವನ್ನು ಕಟ್ಟಿದನು: ನೀರಿನಂತೆ ಕಾಣುವ ಹೊಳಪುಳ್ಳ ಸ್ಫಟಿಕ ನೆಲಗಳು, ಮತ್ತು ಘನ ನೆಲದಂತೆ ಕಾಣುವ ಸ್ವಚ್ಛ ನೀರಿನ ಕೊಳಗಳು, ಇದರಿಂದ ಕಣ್ಣಿಗೆ ಘನವಾದುದನ್ನೂ ಕಾಣಿಸುವುದನ್ನೂ ಬೇರ್ಪಡಿಸಲಾಗಲಿಲ್ಲ. ಅದೊಂದು ಅದ್ಭುತವಾಗಿತ್ತು; ಮತ್ತು ಅದು, ಇನ್ನೂ ಯಾರಿಗೂ ತಿಳಿಯದಿದ್ದರೂ, ಒಂದು ಬಲೆಯೂ ಆಗಿತ್ತು, ಏಕೆಂದರೆ ಅದು ಇಂದ್ರಿಯಗಳ ಮೇಲೆ ಉಂಟುಮಾಡಿದ ಗೊಂದಲವು ಒಂದು ದಿನ ಒಬ್ಬ ಅಹಂಕಾರಿ ಅತಿಥಿಯ ಮೇಲೆ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡಿ ಜಗತ್ತನ್ನು ಸುಡುವ ಜಗಳವನ್ನು ಹೊತ್ತಿಸುತ್ತದೆ. ಆದರೆ ಅದು ಮುಂದೆ ಇತ್ತು. ಈಗಂತೂ ಮರುಪಡೆದ ಮರುಭೂಮಿ ಒಂದು ಪ್ರಕಾಶಮಾನ ನಗರವಾಗಿ ಮಾರ್ಪಟ್ಟಿತು ಮಾತ್ರ, ಮತ್ತು ಪಾಂಡವರು ತಮ್ಮ ಅದೃಷ್ಟದ ಉತ್ತುಂಗದಲ್ಲಿದ್ದರು, ಮತ್ತು ಭ್ರಮೆಯ ಮಂಟಪವು ಅದರ ಹೃದಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಹೊಳೆಯಿತು, ಸುಂದರವಾದ ಮತ್ತು ವಿಶ್ವಾಸಘಾತುಕವಾದ, ಎಷ್ಟು ಘನವಾಗಿ ಕಾಣುತ್ತದೋ ಮತ್ತು ಮೂಲಭೂತವಾಗಿ ವಂಚನೆಯಿಂದ ಕಟ್ಟಲ್ಪಟ್ಟಿದೆಯೋ ಅಂತಹ ಸಂಪೂರ್ಣ ಭೌತಿಕ ವೈಭವಕ್ಕೆ ತಕ್ಕ ಸಂಕೇತ.10:T18e2,जब यह ज्ञात हुआ कि पांडव मोम के महल के जलने से बच निकले हैं, और वे जीवित होकर हस्तिनापुर लौट आए हैं, तो उत्तराधिकार का संकट खड़ा हो गया, क्योंकि अब उत्तराधिकारियों के दो समूह थे, और धृतराष्ट्र, दुर्योधन के प्रति अपने प्रेम और अपने कर्तव्य के बीच सदा की तरह फटे हुए, ने राज्य को विभाजित करके इसका समाधान किया। और वह विभाजन स्वयं एक अपमान था, क्योंकि पांडवों को उसने खांडवप्रस्थ दिया, एक बंजर मरुभूमि क्षेत्र और प्राचीन बंजर भूमि, वीरान और अवांछित, जबकि दुर्योधन ने राज्य के समृद्ध और बसे हुए हृदय को अपने पास रखा।
