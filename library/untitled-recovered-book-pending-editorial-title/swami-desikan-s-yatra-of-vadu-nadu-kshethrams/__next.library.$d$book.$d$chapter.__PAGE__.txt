5:T470,Swami Deshikan's Yātrā of Vadu Nadu Kshethrams

In the year 1317 AD Swami Deshikan and his wife Kanakavalli were blessed with a son named Varadacharya. Just like his father, Varadacharya was a learned soul. It was as though a lamp was lit from an already bright and shining lamp. Soon after the birth of Varadacharya, Swami Deshikan undertook a yatra of all the Vadu Nadu Kshethrams. The first stop made by Swami Deshikan was that at Tirumala. Here, Swami Deshikan composed the Dayā Shatakam, a hundred verse treatise on the characteristics of the Lord's quality of showering limitless daya or mercy on all of His devotees. When Swami Deshikan presented this work in front of Lord Srinivasa, the Lord is said to have been so moved by the rendition that He conferred Swami Deshikan with the title of Vedantaacharya.

Swami Deshikan then continued his journey north and visited many kshethrams such as Tirukadigai, Tiruayodhyi, Kāshī, Mathurā, Avantika, Badarikashramam, Puri, Brindavan, Dwārakā, Triveni, Gaya, Salagramam, Purushothamam, Srikoormam, etc. Before returning to Kanchi, he also visited Tiruvallikkeni and Tiruvallur.6:Tc85,ஸ்வாமி தேசிகனின் வடு நாடு க்ஷேத்திரங்களின் யாத்திரை

ईस्वी सन् 1317 में स्वामी देशिकन् और उनकी पत्नी कनकवल्ली को वरदाचार्य नामक एक पुत्र प्राप्त हुआ। अपने पिता के समान ही, वरदाचार्य भी एक विद्वान आत्मा थे। मानो पहले से ही प्रकाशमान एक दीपक से एक और दीपक प्रज्वलित किया गया हो। वरदाचार्य के जन्म के तुरंत बाद, स्वामी देशिकन् ने समस्त वडु नाडु क्षेत्रम् की यात्रा की। स्वामी देशिकन् का पहला पड़ाव तिरुमला में था। यहाँ, स्वामी देशिकन् ने, अपने समस्त भक्तों पर असीम दया अथवा करुणा बरसाने वाले भगवान के गुण की विशेषताओं पर सौ पद्यों का ग्रंथ दया शतकम् रचा। स्वामी देशिकन् ने जब इस कृति को भगवान श्रीनिवास के समक्ष प्रस्तुत किया, तो उस पाठ से इतने प्रभावित हुए भगवान ने स्वामी देशिकन् को वेदांताचार्य की उपाधि प्रदान की, ऐसा कहा जाता है।

तत्पश्चात स्वामी देशिकन् ने उत्तर की ओर अपनी यात्रा जारी रखी और तिरुक्कडिगै, तिरुअयोध्यि, काशी, मथुरा, अवंतिका, बदरिकाश्रमम्, पुरी, बृंदावन, द्वारका, त्रिवेणी, गया, साळग्रामम्, पुरुषोत्तमम्, श्रीकूर्मम् जैसे अनेक क्षेत्रों का दर्शन किया। काञ्ची लौटने से पूर्व, उन्होंने तिरुवल्लिक्केणि और तिरुवल्लूर् का भी दर्शन किया।8:T685,Swami Deshikan's Return to Kanchipuram

After composing Hayagriva Stotram, Swami Deshikan composed various other stotrams, such as Garuḍa Panchashath, a fifty-shloka treatise on Lord Garuḍa, Devanaayaka Panchashath, a fifty-shloka treatise on Devanatha Perumāl, and Vaikuntanaayaki Thaayar, Achyutha Shathakam, a hundred-verse stotram dealing with various ideas of philosophy. Swami Deskian has written Achyutha Shathakam using Naayika Bhavam. Naayika Bhaavam is the process of writing where one imagines oneself as a woman who is in love with the Lord. Swami Deshikan composed the above stotrams and various other magnificent pieces before returning to Kanchipuram. On the way to Kanchipuram, Swami Deshikan first made a stop at Tirukkovilur. At Tirukkovilur, he composed Dehaleesa Sthuthi. Dehaleesa Sthuthi is a stotram in praise of Trivikrama Perumāl, the presiding deity of this nadu naadu Divyadesam as seen through the eyes of the mudhal azhwars, namely Poigai Āzhwār, Bhoothaazhwar, and Pei Āzhwār. Upon returning to Kanchipuram, Swami Deshikan composed Nyasa Dashakam, a ten-verse stotram on the doctrines of prapatti. Another magnum opus composed by Swami Deshikan is the Artha Panchakam.

The Artha Panchakam is a set of 11 Pāsurams dedicated to Sri Varadaraja Perumāl of Kanchipuram. The 11 Pāsurams are assigned to two each of the five doctrines or truths of the Artha Panchakam.

The five doctrines discussed  are as follows:

1. The Svarūpam of Parabrahman.
2. The Svarūpam of the jeevaathma.
3. The obstacles that prevent us from reaching Paramapadam
4. The means to moksham: Bhakti and Prapatti

ಕಾಂಚೀಪುರಂನಲ್ಲಿ, ಒಬ್ಬ ಹಾವಾಡಿಗ ಕೆಲವು ಹಾವುಗಳನ್ನು ತಂದು, ದೇಶಿಕನ್ ಅವುಗಳನ್ನು ನಿಯಂತ್ರಿಸಬಲ್ಲರೇ ಎಂದು ಕೇಳಿದನು. ದೇಶಿಕನ್ ನೆಲದ ಮೇಲೆ ಸೀಮೆಸುಣ್ಣದ ತುಂಡಿನಿಂದ ಒಂದು ಗೆರೆ ಎಳೆದರು. ಹಾವುಗಳಿಗೆ ಆ ಗೆರೆ ದಾಟಲಾಗಲಿಲ್ಲ. ಆದರೆ ಒಂದು ವಿಷಪೂರಿತ ಹಾವು ಅದನ್ನು ದಾಟಿ ಅವರನ್ನು ಬೆದರಿಸಿದಾಗ, ಅವರು ಗರುಡ ಮಂತ್ರವನ್ನು ಉಚ್ಚರಿಸಿದರು. ತಕ್ಷಣ, ಒಂದು ಗರುಡ ಪಕ್ಷಿ ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊಂಡು ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಹಾವುಗಳನ್ನೂ ಎತ್ತಿಕೊಂಡು ಹಾರಿಹೋಯಿತು. ತನ್ನ ಏಕೈಕ ಜೀವನೋಪಾಯವನ್ನು ಕಳೆದುಕೊಂಡ ಹಾವಾಡಿಗ ಕ್ಷಮೆಯಾಚಿಸಿದನು. ದೇಶಿಕನ್ ತಮ್ಮ "ಗರುಡ ದಂಡಕಂ" ಪಠಿಸಿದರು, ಆ ಪಕ್ಷಿ ಹಾವುಗಳನ್ನು ಮರಳಿ ತಂದಿತು.
